# Acme Corp – Company Rules & Policies

## 1. Working Hours
- Standard hours are **08:00–16:00**, Monday to Friday.
- Flexible start is allowed between 07:00 and 09:00, with a matching end time.
- Remote work is permitted up to **2 days per week** with manager approval.

## 2. Vacation & Leave
- Employees are entitled to **25 vacation days** per calendar year.
- Vacation requests must be submitted at least **2 weeks in advance**.
- A maximum of **5 unused days** may be carried over to the next year.
- Sick leave must be reported to your manager before 09:00 on the day of absence.

## 3. Dress Code
- Business casual attire is expected in the office.
- Jeans are permitted on Fridays.
- Client-facing meetings require business formal dress.

## 4. IT & Equipment
- Company laptops may not be used for personal activities.
- All software installations must be approved by the IT department.
- Passwords must be at least 12 characters and changed every 90 days.
- USB drives from unknown sources must never be plugged into company devices.

## 5. Expense Reimbursement
- Business expenses must be submitted within **30 days** of the purchase.
- Receipts are required for any expense over **€20**.
- Meals during business travel are reimbursed up to **€40 per day**.
- Travel bookings must go through the company travel portal.

## 6. Code of Conduct
- All employees must treat colleagues, clients, and partners with respect.
- Harassment or discrimination of any kind will not be tolerated.
- Conflicts of interest must be disclosed to HR immediately.
- Confidential company information must not be shared outside the organization.

## 7. Meetings
- Meetings should have a clear agenda sent at least **24 hours in advance**.
- Meetings should not exceed **1 hour** unless pre-approved.
- The last Friday of each month is a meeting-free day.

## 8. Health & Safety
- Emergency exits must remain clear at all times.
- All workplace injuries must be reported to HR within 24 hours.
- First aid kits are located on every floor near the kitchen area.
